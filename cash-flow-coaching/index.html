A better answer than “just spend less.”

A budget is only helpful when it reflects your real life. We start with your actual income, expenses, upcoming obligations and priorities, then identify what you can reasonably save—and what to adjust if you're falling behind. The point isn't to scrutinize every coffee purchase. It's to make the trade-offs visible so you can decide what matters.

Common questions

Is this the same as financial planning or investment advice?

No. This is a focused budgeting, cash-flow and savings-accountability service. It does not include securities recommendations, investment portfolio management, insurance sales or other regulated investment advisory services.

Do I need to be a tax preparation client?

No. Cash flow coaching can be a separate engagement. Tax preparation is quoted separately unless specifically included in your proposal.

Do you need my banking passwords?

No. Do not email passwords or account credentials. Monarch Money allows clients to connect supported accounts and grant professional access. You can revoke access when you choose.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the scope and frequency of reviews. Email me with your goal and I'll outline the available options before you commit.
